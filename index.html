Our Heritage & Craft

More Than Just Coffee

"At Brew & Bean, we believe great coffee brings people together."

Every cup is carefully prepared using freshly roasted beans and thoughtfully sourced ingredients. Founded in 2020, our mission was born out of a profound passion for the nuanced flavor profiles of Indian and international high-altitude coffee plantations.

From our custom-dialed La Marzocco espresso machines to our slow-filtered ice drips, we bridge traditional warmth with modern artisanal precision. Whether you are stepping in for a morning espresso, an afternoon focus session, or an evening gathering with friends, our doors are always open.
